What the simulation represents

A reproducible grid-resilience simulation testbed. It is not a validated digital twin of a physical feeder. All control actions operate on simulated devices.

How India manages disturbances

Generation response and reserves help balance supply and demand. Operators can manage demand. Emergency protection such as AUFLS disconnects assigned blocks. A local line fault requires isolation and repair even when system frequency is normal. The local-fault preset assumes an ideal stiff upstream grid holding 50 Hz, to isolate service continuity from area-frequency dynamics. Restoration requires available supply and appropriate operator permission.

Normal frequency band: 49.90–50.05 Hz around 50 Hz. CERC IEGC statement of reasons.

Reference AUFLS profile: 49.4 / 49.2 / 49.0 / 48.8 Hz with 5 / 6 / 7 / 7% allocations. This dashboard scales the dated profile to its initial demand and discrete blocks, rather than asserting a specific utility's feeder wiring. SRPC task force, March 2024.

ADMS is separate. A published Southern Region example includes frequency, overdrawal and sustained duration. This short experiment does not reproduce that full logic. Dated ADMS example, 2022.

Fair comparison

Reference AUFLS and AUFLS + Rakshak use identical inventories, generation response, reserves and backup. Rakshak can pause enrolled flexible loads before a feeder stage is needed. It cannot create electricity or power a disconnected home without backup.

DR 49.60 Hz, −0.50 Hz/s predictive trigger, 100 ms relay dwell, 180 s compressor lockout and 47.5 Hz study cutoff are declared assumptions. The cutoff is not a universal generator trip setting. Stage targets are rounded to whole modeled blocks. No voltage or network power-flow model is included.

Why appliances have different priorities

Critical continuity protects designated life-support and clinical circuits. Essential service protects basic household services by project policy. Ordinary includes TV, cooking and active washing cycles. Flexible includes enrolled comfort AC, storage heating and EV charging. Protected does not mean always consuming rated power. User demand, permission and electrical supply are separate.

Domestic refrigerators remain protected conservatively. Vaccine refrigerators require separate clinical classification. Electrical supply alone does not prove temperature safety.

Research positioning

Oyefeso et al. establish experimental AC coordination and HIL testing. Sinitsyn et al. study TCL synchronization. Zhao et al. establish load-side frequency-control theory. These prior results mean demand response and staggering alone are not new.

Our candidate contribution is auditable, consent-aware service protection and restoration alongside conventional protection. Novelty and field performance remain to be established. NIST guidance explains why physical-system connection and validation matter for digital-twin claims.
